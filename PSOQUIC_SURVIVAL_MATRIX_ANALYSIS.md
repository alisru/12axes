## Part 1: Deep Technical & Structural Audit of the 12 Axes Survey

### 1.1 Current Architecture & Mechanics
The **12 Axes** project (`e:\Vector Field Theory\12axes`) is a full-stack political typology engine (Java 21 / Spring Boot 3.3 backend, React 18 / TypeScript frontend) that evaluates users and catalog entities across **12 linear bipolar axes** (`0` to `100`, with `50.0` as neutral center) using a pool of **240 Likert-scale statements** (20 per axis, balanced 10 `LEFT` / 10 `RIGHT`) plus **5 multiple-choice archetype questions**.

What makes 12 Axes stand out from standard political quizzes (like 8values or the classic Political Compass) is its **unified simulation pipeline** (`profile-audit/`): rather than hand-coding coordinate vectors for its catalogs (+260 ideologies, +170 countries/historical regimes, +390 personalities), every profile is generated by having an LLM answer all 240 questions + 5 archetype questions in character, computing the resulting vector via the exact same formula (`ScoringService.java` / `profile_vector.py`) applied to human users.

#### Core Mathematical Components in Production:
1. **Linear Additive Axis Scoring (`ScoringService.java`, `profile_vector.py`)**:
   - Responses map to $s \in \{0.0, 0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 1.0\}$. If `agreePole == RIGHT`, the score is inverted ($1 - s$).
   - Each of the 5 archetype questions (`sociedade`, `poder`, `economia`, `mundo`, `tecnologia`) injects synthetic answers (weight $1.0$) into 1 to 3 axes.
   - Each axis score is a flat arithmetic mean: $\text{Axis}_k = \frac{\sum w_i x_i}{\sum w_i} \times 100$.
2. **Four-Term Profile Compatibility (`ProfileMatchScorer.java`)**:
   - **Per-Axis Similarity ($42\%$ weight)**: Quadratic decay $1 - (\Delta / 50)^2$, multiplied by a hyperbolic tangent opposite-side penalty $1 - 0.45 \tanh(|u - 50|/25)\tanh(|t - 50|/25)$ when user and target sit on opposite sides of $50$.
   - **Augmented Cosine Direction ($33\%$ weight)**: Cosine similarity around the $(50,\dots,50)$ origin, damped by a constant bias `augment` $= 12 \times 8.0^2 = 768$ so near-center vectors do not produce random angular noise.
   - **Magnitude Similarity ($18\%$ weight)**: Compares average distance from center ($100 - 2|\bar{d}_u - \bar{d}_t|$) so moderates do not match extremists who share the same direction vector.
   - **Outlier Penalty ($7\%$ weight)**: Penalizes the single worst-matching axis via $1 - (\max \Delta / 100)^{2.5}$.
3. **2D Political Compass Reduction (`politicalCompass.ts`, `compass_check.py`)**:
   - Projects 8 of the 12 axes onto a $9 \times 9$ grid (`right` $\times$ `authoritarian`):
     $$\text{right}_{\text{raw}} = 0.20(100 - \text{economia}) + 0.30(100 - \text{controle}) + 0.20(100 - \text{comercio}) + 0.25(\text{imigracao}) + 0.05(100 - \text{moral})$$
     stretched via an asymmetric $\tanh$ function (`GAIN_LEFT = 1.6`, `GAIN_RIGHT = 2.6`).
     $$\text{authoritarian} = \frac{\text{poder} + (100 - \text{representacao}) + (100 - \text{estrutura})}{3}$$
   - Completely drops `religiao`, `tecnologia`, `diplomacia`, and `intervencao` from the compass projection.

2. **The "False Opposite" & Dead-Center Collapse (`profile-audit/README.md` Failure Modes #1 & #3)**:
   - Every axis is a single 1D line where rejecting Pole A mathematically forces endorsement of Pole B, and answering `N` (Neutral) injects `0.50`, averaging out to a fake "moderate centrist" (`50.0`).
   - As documented in `README.md`, **Nietzsche** rejected both socialism *and* bourgeois shopkeeper capitalism, so the audit initially scored him as a Rothbardian free-marketeer (`economia: 20`, `controle: 22.5`). **Lassallean Socialism** was scored as a police state (`poder: 66.2`) because it favored state economic aid, conflating economic agency with police coercion.
   - Similarly, someone who opposes *state* central banking (`controle_03`) is forced toward the same pole as someone who wants *corporate deregulation* (`controle_04`), conflating anti-state mutualism with plutocratic extraction.

3. **Conflation of Rhetoric (Ideal) vs. Actuality (Regime Practice) (`README.md` Failure Mode #5)**:
   - In `README.md` ("País respondido pelo ideal da sociedade, não pelo regime"), countries like DR Congo, Kenya, Nigeria, and Tunisia scored `48–87` on Democracy because subagents read normative questions ("Free elections should be maintained...") through constitutional rhetoric rather than institutional reality.
   - Because each profile has only **one vector**, the system cannot store **Stated Ideal vs. Pragmatic Actuality** ($\Delta H$, the Hypocrisy Gap). A regime that *claims* democratic representation while practicing autocratic extraction has its lie erased into a single static number.

4. **Scale Blindness (Confusing Micro, Meso, Macro, and Total)**:

5. **Dimension Matcher Redundancy (`DimensionMatcherService.java` Lines 31–40)**:
   - `DimensionMatcherService` claims to match users across three distinct dimensions: `POLITICAL`, `SOCIAL`, and `ECONOMIC`.
   - Yet in the code:
     - `POLITICAL_AXES` includes **all 12 axes** (`estrutura`, `representacao`, `poder`, `diplomacia`, `imigracao`, `intervencao`, `tecnologia`, `controle`, `comercio`, `religiao`, `economia`, `moral`) — making "Political Match" 100% identical to the overall 12-axis match (it only returns a different person because `excludeId` hides the #1 overall match!).
     - `SOCIAL_AXES` includes **9 of the 12 axes** (including `economia`, `controle`, and `comercio`!).
     - Only `ECONOMIC_AXES` (`economia`, `controle`, `comercio`) is actually a distinct sub-dimension.

6. **Documentation / Implementation Polarity Inversion (`axes-explained.md` vs. `validate.py`)**:
   - As `validate.py` (line 60) and `profile-audit/README.md` (line 299) warn, `axes-explained.md` documents `religiao` and `imigracao` with inverted poles compared to the actual data vectors (`0–100`), creating an ongoing trap for contributors and auditors.

7. **Linear Averaging Hides Systemic Collapse (`AxisTensionService.java` vs. Reality)**:
   - `AxisTensionService` treats an unusual pair of axes (e.g., high `poder` surveillance + low `economia` public safety net, or zero `representacao` + zero `poder` civil liberty) as a quirky statistical curiosity ("Axis Tension: only 3 of 261 ideologies share this pair").
