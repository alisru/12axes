# /kanon_build <nation_or_polity>

Builds a **343 Kanon** — the complete $7 \times 7 \times 7$ structural map of a nation's or polity's soul across **7 Planes**, **7 Senses**, and **7 Vectors**, anchored to verbatim primary sources, **Psochic Hegemony $(\upsilon, \psi)$ coordinates**, and the **$7 \times 4$ Survival Matrix**.

## Why This Exists in `12axes`

In `12axes`, country and historical regime profiles (`countries-profiles.json`) are generated by having an LLM roleplay 240 generic policy statements in character (`profile-audit/README.md`). That approach suffers from five documented structural failures:
1. **Zero Primary Sources**: Not a single quote, law, constitution, or date is stored or verified.
2. **Conflation of Ideal vs. Regime Practice**: Normative questions cause authoritarian regimes to score as democracies unless patched by prompt hacks (`profile-audit/README.md`, Failure Mode #5).
3. **Plane Imbalance**: 67% of the 240 questions only measure Plane 5 (*How* / State Coercion) and Plane 3 (*Where* / Property Ownership), leaving Plane 2 (*What* / Purpose), Plane 4 (*Why* / Refreshment), and Plane 7 (*Effect* / Sustenance) virtually unmeasured (`< 3%` each).
4. **Scale Blindness**: 80.8% of questions only measure the `MACRO` state level, ignoring `MICRO` (somatic), `MESO` (domestic/communal), and `TOTAL` (biospheric/metaphysical) survival.
5. **Erasure of Marginalized / First Nations Realities**: A single 12-number vector erases the difference between settler-state rhetoric and indigenous/marginalized reality.

The **343 Kanon** solves all five by mapping every coordinate of a nation's identity with primary-source evidence and dual-vector accountability.

## 2. The Psochic Hegemony Coordinate System $(\upsilon, \psi)$

Every Kanon vector is scored on two continuous axes from `-2.0` to `+2.0`:

### Axis $\upsilon$ (Morality — Who does this benefit?)
- `+2.0` — **Everyone / All Beings** (Systemic Justice)
- `+1.0` — **Other People / A Being** (Greater Good)
- ` 0.0` — **No One / Neutral**
- `-1.0` — **My Group Only** (Lesser Evil / Tribal or Partisan Exclusion)
- `-2.0` — **Only Me / Pure Extraction** (Tyranny / Elite Capture)

### Axis $\psi$ (Will — What is the energy doing?)
- `+2.0` — **Actively creating systemic value for all** (Productive Justice)
- `+1.0` — **Proactive** (creating, building, acting)
- ` 0.0` — **Neutral** (no meaningful force applied / stasis)
- `-1.0` — **Passive** (allowing, suppressing, withholding)
- `-2.0` — **Actively destroying or extracting value** (Chaos / Collapse)

### Structural Modifier Laws:
1. **`WHERE` modifies $\upsilon$**: Energy injected into a structurally corrupt or exclusionary domain pulls $\upsilon$ negative regardless of noble stated intent.
2. **`HOW` modifies $\psi$**: Coercive, violent, or deceptive methods force $\psi$ negative regardless of stated purpose.
3. **The $0.5$-Zone Perceptual Inversion Warning**: Near the self-serving end of the spectrum, the ego inverts perception: *Extraction feels like Strength/Liberty; Service feels like Burden; Stability feels like Rot; Cruelty feels like Moral Protection.* Flag any vector where stated rhetoric inverts the calculated $(\upsilon, \psi)$ coordinate.

### Zone Classifications:
- **Greater Good**: $(+\upsilon, +\psi)$
- **Greatest Lie**: $(-\upsilon, +\psi)$ — Proactive mobilizing energy serving a narrow/extractive beneficiary.
- **Lesser Good**: $(+\upsilon, -\psi)$ — Universal moral intent undermined by passive withholding or inaction.
- **Greater Evil**: $(-\upsilon, -\psi)$ — Extractive beneficiary paired with destructive or suppressive force.
- **Tension Point**: Mixed or contested historical trajectory.
- **Constraint**: Neutral environmental or geographical boundary condition.

## 4. Evidence & Research Protocol (Hypothesis-Then-Verify)

1. **Zero Hallucination / Zero Paraphrase Rule**:
   - Every `canonical_quote` must be a real, verbatim quote, constitutional article, statute, or dated primary record.
   - Never use training memory directly as a citation.
2. **Two-Step Hypothesis-Then-Verify Sequence**:
   - **Step A (Hypothesis)**: From historical knowledge, identify the specific speech, founding document, law, or event that embodies the mechanism of `Plane.Sense.Vector`.
   - **Step B (Verification)**: Fetch the exact primary source (parliamentary record, official archive, legislation) to verify the verbatim text, date, and speaker before writing the JSON entry. Never search search-engines using poetic vector names—search for the concrete historical mechanism and exact phrases.
3. **First Nations / Marginalized Perspective Shadowing**:
   - Where a mainstream national myth or state institution erases an indigenous or subjugated population, add a second entry at the **same `address`** with `"[First Nations Perspective]"` (or `"[Subaltern Shadow]"`) appended to `name`, carrying its own primary quote, its own $(\upsilon, \psi)$ coordinates, and its own actuality audit.
